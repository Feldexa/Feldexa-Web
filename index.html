FELDEXA · ANDROID

Forms that stay yours.

Feldexa is building a calm, privacy-first way to turn scanned, non-fillable PDF forms into reusable smart forms.

Currently in development

Import a form, review automatically suggested fields, fill it on your device, and export a usable PDF. The core workflow is designed for local processing without uploading document content.

Android first

Designed for Android 12 and later, with German and English support planned for V1.

Privacy by design

PDFs, OCR text, answers, and signatures are intended to remain on the device during the core workflow.

Planned model

Freemium with an optional one-time purchase for watermark-free exports. No subscription is planned for V1.
