# Privacy Principles

Feldexa is being designed around on-device, offline-first document processing.

- The core workflow is intended to work without an Internet connection.
- Core document processing does not require uploading PDFs, OCR text, fields, answers, or signatures.
- Technical observability is limited to diagnostics and performance signals; document content is not intended for telemetry.
- Account and billing concepts are separate from document storage. The public companion site does not process documents or payments.

These are public product principles, not a substitute for the final in-app privacy notice and Play Data Safety declaration.
