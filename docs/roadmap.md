# Public Roadmap

Feldexa is currently in foundation and product-definition work.

Planned high-level sequence:

1. Android foundation and secure local document model.
2. PDF import, rendering, analysis, and field review.
3. Guided editing, export, and recovery.
4. Accessibility, localization, privacy, and quality validation.
5. Internal and closed testing before any staged production rollout.

Dates and download availability will be announced only when they are confirmed.
